Proofline

Security

Effective 4 November 2026 · Version 1 · Reviewed every 6 months

Proofline holds evidence that merchants rely on in payment disputes, so it is built to keep data minimal, encrypted, isolated and unalterable. To report a vulnerability, email CONTACT_EMAIL.

Data minimization

  • Only orders that need evidence are stored: agent orders, AI-referred orders, and orders the merchant's rules flag.
  • We never collect card numbers, card security codes, names or addresses.
  • Customer email is stored only when a confirmation email is required, and is deleted after 90 days. Evidence records hold keyed hashes, not identifiers.

Encryption

  • In transit: HTTPS only, for the app, the API, webhooks and the database connection (TLS with certificate verification).
  • At rest: our database (Neon) and storage (Google Cloud) encrypt all data and backups at rest.
  • Application layer: customer emails, Shopify access tokens and raw webhook payloads are additionally encrypted with AES-256-GCM, bound to the merchant and record they belong to.
  • Secrets live in Google Secret Manager, never in code or repositories.

Isolation and integrity

  • Each merchant's data is isolated twice: every query is scoped to the merchant, and Postgres row-level security blocks access to other merchants' rows even if a query forgot to.
  • Evidence is append-only: the database rejects edits and deletions, events form a hash chain per merchant, and each evidence version is signed (Ed25519). Our public key is published at /.well-known/proofline-signing-key so anyone can verify a record.
  • Evidence copies are written once to storage with retention lock and cannot be overwritten or deleted before their retention ends.

Access control

  • Only Proofline's operator has administrative access to production. Every account with access (Google Cloud, Shopify, GitHub, Neon, Resend) requires a strong unique password and two-factor authentication.
  • The running application uses a dedicated service account that can only read its own secrets and create and read evidence objects. It has no permission to delete evidence.
  • Production customer data is never copied to development. Development and tests use synthetic data and development stores.

Logging

  • Google Cloud audit logs record administrative activity and every read of secrets and evidence storage (data access logs).
  • The application keeps an audit log of installs, uninstalls, policy changes, API key changes and privacy requests.
  • Request logs never contain credentials, customer contact details or the tokens in confirmation links; those are masked.

Data loss prevention

  1. Prevent exposure: minimization, encryption and isolation as above; no customer data in logs, email bodies beyond the order summary, or local machines.
  2. Prevent loss: evidence is stored twice (database and write-once storage) and cannot be deleted early; the database supports point-in-time restore.
  3. Detect: integrity checks recompute the evidence hash chain and compare each record with its write-once copy; a billing alert flags unusual usage.
  4. Recover: restore the database to a point in time, recover evidence from its signed write-once copies, and rotate any exposed secret through Secret Manager.

Incident response

An incident is any event that may compromise the confidentiality, integrity or availability of merchant or customer data. The operator is the incident lead.

  1. Report: anyone can report a suspected incident to CONTACT_EMAIL. Alerts and log reviews also open incidents.
  2. Triage (within 24 hours): confirm what happened, which data and which merchants are affected, and rate severity.
  3. Contain: rotate affected secrets (database credentials, Shopify client secret, email key, encryption keys as needed), revoke API keys and tokens, block the attack path, and preserve logs as evidence.
  4. Notify: affected merchants without undue delay and within 48 hours of confirming a breach of their data, with what happened, what data, and what to do; Shopify as its Partner terms require; and authorities where the law requires.
  5. Recover: restore service and data, verify evidence integrity, and watch for recurrence.
  6. Review (within 7 days): write down the cause, the timeline and the fixes, and update this page and our controls.

This plan is rehearsed at least once a year.

Shopify privacy requests

Proofline handles Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks: customer data requests are answered within 30 days, customer redaction deletes stored contact details, and shop redaction deletes the shop's stored contacts and pending notifications. See the Privacy Policy for what is retained and why.
