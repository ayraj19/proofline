    Questions merchants ask

    Will this slow down my checkout?

    No. Proofline listens to order and payment events after they happen. It never sits between your customer and your payment provider.

    Will confirmations cost me sales?

    Defaults are light: only high-value, final-sale and first-time-agent orders. You set the thresholds, and a weekly report shows confirmed versus dropped orders so you can tune them.

    I already use a chargeback service. Does this clash?

    It shouldn’t. You choose who files each dispute. Proofline can hand sealed evidence to your existing vendor instead of filing itself, so nothing gets answered twice.

    What data do you store?

    Order details, the customer’s instruction to their agent, confirmations and delivery proof. Never card numbers. Personal data is minimized and encrypted, and you can export or delete it.

    What happens to my records if I leave?

    You can export every record at any time, including after cancelling, for the rest of its retention period.
